# PRD - Care Home Workforce & Compliance Management System

## Product Vision

A centralized workforce, HR, attendance, visitor, payroll, and compliance platform for care homes.

## Problem Statement

Current rota software only manages shifts and lacks:

* Staffing validation
* Attendance tracking
* Payroll calculations
* Visitor management
* HR document compliance
* Audit tracking

## Objectives

1. Centralize staff operations.
2. Improve staffing visibility.
3. Simplify payroll processing.
4. Ensure compliance readiness.
5. Reduce manual administration.

## User Roles

* Admin
* HR
* Compliance Officer
* Manager
* Receptionist
* Employee

## Core Modules

### 1. Dashboard

Operational overview and KPIs.

### 2. Smart Rota Management

Shift planning and staffing validation.

### 3. Attendance

Clock-in/out and break tracking.

### 4. HR Management

Employee records and onboarding.

### 5. Document Compliance

Document storage, verification, expiry tracking.

### 6. Visitor Management

Visitor registration and logs.

### 7. Leave Management

Holiday allocation and approvals.

### 8. Payroll Reports

Monthly hours and payroll exports.

### 9. Audit Management

Scheduled compliance audits.

## Success Metrics

* Reduced scheduling errors
* Reduced compliance violations
* Faster payroll generation
* Improved audit completion rate
* Accurate staffing coverage
